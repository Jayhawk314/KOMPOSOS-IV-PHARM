KOMPOSOS-IV-PHARM

Drug repurposing that shows its work

Which already-approved drug might also treat this disease — and through what mechanism. A categorical reasoning engine over a curated drug–target–disease graph.

The one job

Find a new use for an existing drug

Repurposing an approved drug skips years of safety work — if you can find the right match and explain why it should work.

  • Rank existing drugs for a disease — with a mechanistic reason.
  • Every candidate traces a path: Drug → Protein → Disease.
  • Not a black box, not an LLM guess — a traceable chain.
The core idea

Drugs, proteins, diseases as a graph you can reason over

Known facts are arrows: drug inhibits protein, protein drives disease. Compose them and you've derived a candidate — with the mechanism attached. Yoneda/Rezk also find drug "look-alikes" (e.g. the MEK or BRAF inhibitor classes).

The data · a knowledge graph

Not a table — a web of facts you can follow

78 drugs · 366 proteins · 20 diseases · 2,178 connections · 44 proven drug→disease links.

A knowledge graph = every node is a thing, every edge a verified fact — so the engine can follow chains, not just look things up.

One way it reasons · Kan extension live · ≈0.20 weight

Best-supported guess from everything around it

Take everything the drug already connects to — its known targets — and combine them (a weighted colimit) into the best-supported extension to a new disease.

Like filling a gap in a map from all the points around it. PHARM really uses this. pointwise Kan extension

The honest part

What's solid — and what isn't

✓ Solid

  • Caught & retired an inflated 0.969 — it had label leakage. The 0.949 is the corrected, isolated number.
  • Beats every classical baseline on the same graph.
  • Every positive has a mechanistic path; candidates come with evidence chains.

~ Limits

  • Research prototype — NOT clinical validation.
  • Small curated graph: 78 drugs, 20 diseases, 44 approved positives.
  • External set (Hetionet) is weaker: AUROC 0.63, low precision-at-top.
  • Provenance is tiered: 594 relation-verified vs 215 co-occurrence — presence ≠ verification.

Saying this is what makes the 0.949 believable.

What you get

Ranked candidates with the receipts

For a disease, it ranks drugs and shows the Drug → Protein → Disease path, the strategy votes, and source-linked evidence — then cross-checks ClinicalTrials.gov (≈63% of top candidates are already in trials, 7% genuinely novel).

A triage tool for "what's worth a closer look," with the reasoning exposed — not a treatment recommendation.

Scope

Two parts

Repurposing

  • New uses for already-approved drugs.
  • The validated part — the numbers above.
  • Graph-based: composition + Yoneda/Rezk + Kan.

Drug design

  • Designing and scoring new molecules from scratch.
  • In progress — early, not yet validated.
