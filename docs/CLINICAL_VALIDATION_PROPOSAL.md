# Clinical Validation Partnership Proposal

## What You Would Provide

### Option 1: Retrospective Validation (Easiest)
- Review top candidates for your cancer area
- Check against your clinical trials database
- Identify which are already in trials, published, or off-label use
- Mark truly novel vs. already-explored candidates

**Time**: 2-4 hours
**Deliverable**: Classification of our candidates (known/trial/published/novel)

### Option 2: Mechanistic Review (Medium)
- Assess biological plausibility of top 10-20 candidates
- Identify which mechanisms are cancer-relevant
- Suggest missing pathways or contraindications
- Flag candidates worth pursuing vs. unlikely to work

**Time**: 4-8 hours
**Deliverable**: Prioritized candidate list with mechanistic assessment

### Option 3: Experimental Validation (High Value)
- Test top 3-5 candidates in cell lines or animal models
- Validate predicted mechanisms (target inhibition, pathway effects)
- Measure efficacy at clinically-relevant doses
- Co-author publication on validated candidates

**Time**: 3-6 months
**Deliverable**: Experimental validation paper, co-authorship
